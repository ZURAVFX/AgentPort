## Chrome Web Store short description
Send the current live webpage to After Effects as editable text, shapes and image layers. Local-only and open source.

## Chrome / Firefox long description
Web2AE turns the webpage you are currently viewing into an animation-ready After Effects composition.

Click the Web2AE browser button and the extension reads the page's real rendered structure: text, element geometry, styles, images, controls and grouping. The local After Effects companion then rebuilds the visible viewport using editable AE text and shape layers wherever practical, while keeping complex visuals as tightly bounded pixel-accurate layers.

Highlights:
• Live webpage → After Effects in one click after setup
• Editable AE text for compatible typography
• Native shape layers for common cards, buttons, fills and borders
• Separate thumbnails, avatars, images and visual elements
• Rounded corners and clipping preserved where possible
• Smart semantic precomps without breaking browser paint order
• Works with the page state you can actually see, including dynamic/logged-in pages
• Chrome, Edge and Firefox support
• No AI API, credits, subscription or cloud processing
• Free and open source

Web2AE only processes a page when you explicitly press Send Current Page to AE. Capture data is sent to the Web2AE companion on your own computer at 127.0.0.1 and is not uploaded to the developer.

Requires the free Web2AE After Effects companion and Adobe After Effects 2024+ on Windows.
